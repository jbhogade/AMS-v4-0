Applies to every printed report and the Asset Issue/Handover Forms. Remembered in this browser.

Per-employee asset holdings: Direct = personally held (no subordinate/team holder recorded); Team = held by the employee's subordinates, or assigned to the employee with a subordinate/team member as the actual user.
